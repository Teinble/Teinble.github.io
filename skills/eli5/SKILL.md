# ELI5

Explain the idea for a curious beginner without losing the important truth.

## Explanation Shape

1. Start with the simplest accurate explanation in plain language.
2. Give one familiar analogy.
3. Walk through one small concrete example.
4. Introduce the proper technical terms after the intuition is clear.

Keep the explanation respectful rather than childish. Avoid jargon until it is defined. If the analogy breaks down in an important way, say where it stops matching reality.
